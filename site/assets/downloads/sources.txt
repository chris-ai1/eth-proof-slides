Authors: Christian Reitwiessner
Mathematical sources retain their original authorship.
Sources read 5 November 2026. Source PDFs are in the parent research/ directory.

Kintali
https://shivakintali.github.io/papers/ETH.pdf
Main proof route; precise fixed-parameter recovery, spectral construction, schedules, and bit arithmetic.

Sellke
https://msellke.com/Files/ETH-False-Slides.pdf
Visual motivation: group separation, counting deformation, swaps, and interpolation.

O’Donnell
https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~odonnell/some_eth_false_exposition.pdf
Cross-check of the parallel matrix/permanent route; it is not used as a black-box reduction in this talk.

OpenAI manuscript
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/openai/math/main/preprints/The-Exponential-Time-Hypothesis-is-False-October-25-2026/paper.pdf
Original reported result; its sharper runtime and general counting/space claims are outside this presentation.

Community examination
https://ethdiscussion.blog/
Context and links. Examination organizers distinguish organizing scrutiny from certifying a result.
