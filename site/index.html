What is shown, what is stated.

This is an exposition of the reported direct SAT argument. The spectral and placement results are stated technical lemmas. Group separation and Hermite uniqueness are explained; small-support recovery is demonstrated, with the general bound and exact-arithmetic estimates stated.

The quantified companion includes the full hypotheses and supplementary spectral construction. This site does not claim an independent formal audit or a practical SAT speedup.
